# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.

Be respectful, assume good intent, accept constructive feedback, and avoid language or behavior that targets people or groups. Project maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately to the organization owners through GitHub. This policy is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
